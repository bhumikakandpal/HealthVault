## 📖 About The Project

HealthVault is a web-based **Personal Health Record Organizer** designed to make family medical record management easier and more organized.

Medical documents such as prescriptions, laboratory reports, and other health records are often scattered across different files and platforms. Finding the right information when needed can become difficult.

HealthVault aims to provide a centralized platform where families can organize and access their health records conveniently.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Families often face difficulties in:

- 📄 Managing scattered medical documents and prescriptions.
- 🔍 Finding previously stored medical records.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Managing health information of multiple family members.
- 📋 Keeping medical history organized for doctor visits.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

HealthVault aims to simplify medical record management through a centralized digital platform.

It provides an organized interface to view family health information, manage records, and access health summaries.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 1. Dashboard
- Overview of available family health information.
- Organized presentation of existing demo records.
- Centralized access to different sections.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 2. Family Records
- View existing demo family members.
- Access records associated with individual members.

### 📁 3. Record Management
- Add records to existing family members.
- View individual record details.

### 📤 4. Upload Page
- Dedicated interface for managing medical record uploads.

### 📋 5. Health Summary
- Dedicated page for viewing organized health information.

*Note: Features listed above reflect the current prototype structure. Actual functionality may vary.*
